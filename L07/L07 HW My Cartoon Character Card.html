About My Character

Character Name: Ashlyn Banner

Best Friend: Taylor Hernandez

Favourite hobby: Ballet

Special Power: Very good hearing

Appearance

Ashlyn has long ginger braids and many freckles. Her expression is most to always cold or deadpan, and she's of short height. She has slate green eyes and is often seen wearing black earplugs or headphones to cancel out any noise.

Personality

Ashlyn doesn't like to talk or interact with people outside her family. As noted by Taylor and her father, Ashlyn hasn't made any friends in her many years of living and was always alone at school prior to the events of the comic. Due to not having much social interaction, Ashlyn can be abrasive and blunt. She is quick to take action when necessary and is more than willingly to stand up for herself. Ashlyn loves ballet and that is the one thing she tends to find herself doing alone in her room ever since she messed up at a recital once.
